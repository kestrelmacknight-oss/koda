What Koda does

Built for the way you actually communicate.

Everything you need to run your community — without handing your conversations to a corporation.

🔐

Private by default

Every message in every channel is end-to-end encrypted. Your conversations live on your devices — not in a company database being read, analysed, or sold to advertisers. Private is the default, not a premium setting.

🏘

Servers & channels

Create a server for your guild, your stream community, or your group of friends. Build text channels, voice channels, and subscriber-only areas — all with proper role and permission controls.

🎙

Private voice & video

Squad calls, team strats, co-streams, community watch parties — your voice sessions are private too. What happens in your voice channel stays in your voice channel.

💬

Encrypted direct messages

One-on-one DMs between you and your contacts are fully private. Safety numbers let you verify you’re talking to who you think you are — important when managing a large community.

💰

Creator tools

Subscription tiers, digital product sales, subscriber-only channels. Run your community economy directly inside Koda — keep your audience close and your revenue stream steady.

🛡

Community safety

Robust moderation tools, server audit logs, content ratings, parental controls, and role-based permissions — because privacy doesn’t mean unmoderated. Your server, your rules.

Privacy that actually means something

Your messages are yours. Full stop.

Most platforms encrypt messages in transit — meaning they’re encrypted between your device and the company’s server, where they get decrypted and stored in plain text. That’s not private. That’s a filing cabinet with a padlock on the front door.

Koda encrypts messages on your device before they leave. Our servers store encrypted data we can’t read. We’re not going to scan your conversations to serve you ads, report you to advertisers, or make your content decisions for you.

  • ✓Messages encrypted before leaving your device
  • ✓No one at Koda can read your messages or hear your calls
  • ✓Verify your contacts’ identity to prevent impersonation
  • ✓Two-factor authentication with backup codes
  • ✓No advertising. No behavioural tracking. No data selling.

Private voice channels

Your squad calls stay between your squad.

When you’re coordinating a raid, planning a stream, or just hanging out on a Friday night — that conversation is nobody’s business but yours. Koda’s voice is private from the ground up.

The server routes encrypted audio it can’t decode. No one at Koda, no advertiser, no algorithm is listening to your calls and using what you say to target you.

  • Private voice for squads, guilds, and stream communities
  • Video calls and screen sharing built in
  • Noise suppression, echo cancellation, push-to-talk
  • Pop voice out — talk while browsing other channels
  • No one outside your channel can hear a word
